How this desk works

Different places earn different kinds of claims

Yaquina Head can have a managing-agency discovery window. Haystack Rock uses a local tide threshold. Hug Point is a lower-tide access problem without a defensible exact cutoff. Thor's Well remains conditions-only until its spectacle behavior is calibrated well enough to support a production window.

Those differences are intentional. The product does not turn unlike evidence into a single green/yellow/red score, and it does not label coastal conditions safe.

Source discipline: If official guidance conflicts, goes stale or cannot be refreshed, the product says so. It does not silently substitute a weaker tide rule.
