How to use this

When should you actually go?

GO NOW is only issued when physical conditions are favorable and automated access verification is available. CHECK ACCESS means the spectacle model can run but NPS access could not be automatically verified. HAZARDOUS CONDITIONS or CLOSED overrides the trip recommendation even when Thunder Potential is high.

At Thunder Hole, bigger is not automatically better. NPS warns about rogue waves, wet granite and storm surf. Stay behind railings and follow posted closures.

Quick answers

Thunder Hole timing FAQ

What is the best time to visit Thunder Hole?

The National Park Service recommends roughly one to two hours before high tide for the best chance of hearing the boom. This tool refines that window using actual wave energy because the right tide can still be quiet when the ocean is calm.

Is high tide itself best?

Not necessarily. NPS guidance points to the rising-tide period before high tide, and the model uses a smooth timing curve centered in that pre-high window.

Can a high score be unsafe?

Yes. Strong surf can increase the spectacle and the danger at the same time. The physical score and Visit Status are intentionally separate.

Why is the score not a percent chance?

There is not enough direct outcome data to calibrate a true probability. The 0–100 number is an opportunity index, not an 84% claim.
